Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2025

1. Introduction

SOLace ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by SOLace when you use our educational testing platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collection by Age Group

Our information collection practices differ based on the user's age to ensure COPPA compliance:

2.2 Information from Users Under 13

We do NOT collect any personally identifying information from users under or over the age of 13, including:

  • Names, usernames, or display names
  • Email addresses or contact information
  • Photos or images
  • Location information (beyond country)
  • Device identifiers that could identify a specific child
  • Any other personal information as defined by COPPA
  • Test progress or scores

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
  • Track and analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Monitor and analyze the effectiveness of our Service
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent requests and other illegal activities

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, data analysis, customer service, and email delivery.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

Our data retention practices vary by user age:

6.1 Users Under 13

Since we do not collect personal information from users under 13, there is no personal data to retain on our servers. Any educational progress or scores are stored locally on the user's device only.

6.2 Users 13 and Older

We retain personal information from users 13 and older for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Test results and performance data may be retained to provide ongoing progress tracking.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured format
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your personal information
  • Objection: Object to our processing of your personal information

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our use of cookies and similar tracking technologies varies by user age group:

8.1 Users Under 13

We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies to collect personal information from users under the age of 13. Any cookies used for users under 13 are strictly functional (such as maintaining accessibility preferences locally) and do not collect or transmit personal information.

8.2 Users 13 and Older

For users 13 and older, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and use personal information about you. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of our Service.

8.3 Parental Control

Parents can control cookie settings in their child's browser to further limit any data collection. We recommend parents review their browser's privacy settings and consider using parental control software if desired.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SOLace is designed as an educational platform suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

9.1 Information Collection from Children Under 13

We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. Our practices for users under 13 include:

  • No Account Registration Required: Children under 13 can use SOLace's core educational features without creating an account
  • No Personal Information Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers from users under 13
  • Local Data Storage Only: Test progress and scores for users under 13 are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to our servers
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not use cookies or similar technologies to track the online activities of users under 13
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Since we don't collect personal information from children under 13, no such information is shared with third parties

9.2 Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians of children under 13 have the right to:

  • Review any personal information we may have collected about their child (though we do not collect such information)
  • Request that we delete any information about their child
  • Refuse to allow any further collection or use of their child's information
  • Contact us with questions about our privacy practices regarding children

9.3 Parental Consent

If we ever change our practices to collect personal information from children under 13, we will first obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting any such information.

9.4 Contact for Child Privacy Concerns

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's privacy or believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at [YOUR EMAIL] with "Child Privacy" in the subject line.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your jurisdiction where privacy laws may differ. By using our Service, you consent to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@learnsolace.org.

13. Additional Rights for EU Residents

If you are a resident of the European Union, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. Additional Rights for California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.
