Why Choose SOLace?

About SOLace

SOLace was created with a simple mission: to provide high-quality educational assessments that are accessible to everyone, especially young learners who need safe, privacy-focused platforms. Learn more about our goals and team here.

COPPA Compliant

We don't collect any personal information from users under 13, ensuring complete compliance with children's privacy laws.

Educator Approved

Developed in consultation with teachers and education specialists to meet real classroom needs.

Continuously Updated

Regular content updates and new features based on user feedback and educational best practices.
