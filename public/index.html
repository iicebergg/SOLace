About SOLace

SOLace was created by a student with a simple mission: to provide high-quality SOL test preparation materials that are accessible to everyone, especially young learners who need safe, privacy-focused platforms. Learn more about SOLace's goals here.

COPPA Compliant

No personally identifying information is ever collected from users under 13, ensuring complete compliance with children's privacy laws.

Educator Approved

Developed in consultation with teachers and school staff to meet real classroom needs.

Continuously Updated

Regular content updates and new features based on user feedback and improvements to standards.
